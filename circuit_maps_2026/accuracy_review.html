F1Sim2D — circuit geometry review

All 24 game circuits use geographic GP centerlines and independent pit roads, with FIA start/control landmarks and turn numbering. Geographic aspect ratios are preserved. Grid slots are eight metres apart, sixteen metres in the same column.

These maps are reference-matched approximations, not survey models. Fourteen circuits use spatially matched satellite-derived width variation above the FIA reference width. Others retain FIA reference widths; Madrid uses the existing nominal width. Pit speed lines and garage allocations remain simulation placements. Aero/OVT zones are simulation-generated, not verified 2026 race-control overlays.

Eighteen main drawings are 2026 editions. Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Brazil, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi use 2025 baselines (six editions); see each source below. Pole sides also use the FIA circuit register and explicitly labelled 2025 pit drawings. The Madrid pole side remains an assumption pending a labelled source.
